Best GTA 6 setup under $800

At this level the console takes most of the money. A 1080p 120Hz or 1440p screen and a decent wired headset beat chasing a 4K panel you can’t afford. If you already own a TV with HDMI 2.1, skip the monitor and spend the money on a wireless headset and a 1TB SSD.

Monitor or TV for a console?

Look for HDMI 2.1, 120Hz and VRR if you want smooth motion on current consoles. A 27in 1440p monitor suits a desk. A 42in to 55in 4K TV suits a couch. Check the exact model’s specs before buying, since many cheap panels list 144Hz but only over DisplayPort.

Do I need 4K 120Hz for GTA 6?

Rockstar hasn’t confirmed performance modes as far as we know, so verify before spending extra. A 4K 120Hz screen future-proofs you for other games, but it costs real money. If your budget is tight, a good 1440p screen is the better value.

Is there a PC version?

At launch the game is announced for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. This builder will add a PC path if a port gets announced.
